I am honored to have been selected as one of this year's Kavli
Fellows! The National Academy of Sceinces names these fellows annually
(since 1989).  [This year, eighty-eight early-career scientists were
named Kavli
Fellows](https://www.nasonline.org/news/national-academy-of-sciences-selects-the-2025-kavli-fellows/),
from across industry, academia, and government. We were invited to
attend the 3-day symposium, the [2025 U.S. Kavli Frontiers of
Science](https://www.nasonline.org/symposia/2025-u-s-kavli-frontiers-of-science/).
This is the broadest-scope conference I've ever attended, and it was
refreshing to hear so much science from outside my tiny niche.
Thanks to the NAS and the Kavli Foundation for including me!
