Congratulations to Aniket Khairnar and Subhayu Bagchi on defending their PhDs!
We had a double-header defense day, with Aniket in the morning and Subhayu in
the afternoon. Three committee members were in common between the two defenses.

![]({{ site.url }}/images/posts/Two-more-PhDs/Aniket-Leo-Subhayu.jpg)

The titles of their theses were "Modeling and Phenomenology of Numerical
Relativity Simulations via Post Newtonian Theory" from Aniket, and "Numerical
Relativity for Theories of Gravity beyond General Relativity" from Subhayu.
This brings my number of PhD students who've defended so far to 6!
