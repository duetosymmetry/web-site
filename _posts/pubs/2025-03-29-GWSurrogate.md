> GWSurrogate is an easy to use python interface to gravitational wave
> surrogate models. Surrogates provide a fast and accurate evaluation
> mechanism for gravitational waveforms which would traditionally be
> found through solving differential equations. Those solutions are
> computed by expensive simulation codes that can take days to months
> to run. Surrogate models offer a practical way to dramatically
> accelerate model evaluation while retaining the high-fidelity
> accuracy of the expensive simulation code. All available models can
> be found in `gwsurrogate.catalog.list()`.
